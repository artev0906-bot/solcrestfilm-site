Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Solcrest Film Co LLC collects when you contact us or request an estimate, how that information is used, and the choices you have. Solcrest Film Co LLC is a window film installation company based in Los Angeles, California.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Information collected

We collect only what you give us and what is needed to answer your request:

  • Contact details you submit — name, phone number, email address and postal code.
  • Project details you choose to share, including your message, the service requested and any photos you send.
  • Whether you opted in to informational or promotional text messages, and when that choice was made.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, device type and referring page.

We do not ask for and do not want payment card numbers, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information through this website.

How information is used

Information you submit is used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and prepare an estimate.
  • Schedule appointments and send installation updates, reminders and service follow-ups.
  • Provide customer support and keep a record of the work performed.
  • Send promotional messages, but only if you separately opted in to receive them.
  • Meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

SMS opt-in details

Text messaging is optional. Consent is not a condition of purchase, and you can request an estimate without agreeing to receive any text messages. Informational and promotional messages are two separate choices, and both are unchecked by default.

  • Informational messages may cover estimates, appointments, installation updates, reminders, and service follow-ups.
  • Promotional messages may cover special offers and seasonal promotions.
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Reply HELP for help.

SMS consent and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may only receive information as necessary to deliver communications and services on our behalf — for example the platform that hosts this website, the service that delivers our forms and the messaging provider that sends our texts. Those providers are permitted to use the information only to perform that work for us, and never for their own marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This page sets no cookies of its own. Elsewhere on solcrestfilm.com we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site, which sets cookies containing a randomly generated identifier. That data is used in aggregate to improve the site.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using the browser add-on Google provides. Blocking cookies does not prevent you from using this site or submitting a form. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell tracking data.

Data security

This site is served over HTTPS, so information you submit is encrypted in transit. Submissions are stored with our hosting and form providers under their own security controls, and access is limited to the people who need it to respond to your request.

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to serve you and to meet business and legal requirements. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we do not collect more than we need and we do not keep it longer than we need.

User rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to records we are required to keep.
  • Withdraw SMS consent by replying STOP to any message, or by contacting us directly.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you altogether.

California residents may exercise the rights available to them under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, the right to delete and the right to opt out of sale — noting that we do not sell personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights. To make a request, use the contact details below.
